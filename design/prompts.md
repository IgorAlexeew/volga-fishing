# Запросы для генерации изображений

Исходные запросы для ImageGen. Общий макет использует присланные снимки садка. Кадр поимки использует общий макет как образец. Для игры фоны берега подготовлены без солнечного диска и дорожки на воде: их рисует освещение сцены.

## Общий макет

```text
Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished complete production screen design sheet for the existing Russian Volga float-fishing game, based on the two supplied keepnet screenshots (reference for content, density and direction). Four precisely aligned landscape screens in a 2x2 grid, large landscape image; thin neutral gutters, orthographic flat UI, no surrounding presentation, no titles outside the screens. Each screen represents a 360×218-pixel VS Code sidebar. Art direction: a Volga angler's well-used canvas tackle wallet: dark olive woven canvas, fine flax stitching, warm lightly mottled fishing log paper labels, patinated brass hardware only on real controls. Crisp single humanist Cyrillic sans-serif. Tactile believable materials, restrained and readable, NOT ornate fantasy or wood carving. Top left full-bleed realistic daylight Volga river, a small red float and line and rod at right, just two tiny square brass/olive icon buttons top right, two very slender vertical gauges low left. Top right 'Садок 5', small close control; two-column tight contiguous grid of horizontal cream specimen labels each fish illustration LEFT and species name and bold weight RIGHT e.g. 'Густера 476 г', 'Плотва 481 г', 'Окунь 447 г'. Blank space below is olive mesh. Bottom left detailed 'Густера' with a lightly grained cream field-note card: prominent natural fish illustration on the left, '476 г' on the right, below short Russian observations and bait; simple faint ruler ticks along specimen illustration, no fake measurements. Bottom right 'Снасть': six illustrated paper labels with small bait pictures, arranged 3 columns and 2 rows, 'Червь', 'Опарыш', 'Мотыль', 'Перловка', 'Мякиш', 'Тесто', selected worm brass check, compact time selector and collapsed fishing instructions below. Avoid large headlines, decorative words, numbers unrelated to gameplay, thick frames or excess padding. Artwork polished, photoreal fish and bait.
```

Each quadrant is independent exactly the same angle, natural features and scale, different times: upper left pale pink misty sunrise, upper right bright blue midday like the reference, lower left warm copper sunset, lower right blue moonlit sky and water. IMPORTANT CHANGE: distinctly SANDY light warm beige local Volga sand shore in left foreground, no riprap and no boulders. Sandy low eroded point under deciduous trees at the left edge projecting about 23% of the frame width, sparse small roots and tufts of reeds. A place to fish CLOSE to shore: water reaches the bottom edge from x=0.18 across to the right, broad sunlit river with fine natural ripples in foreground. Reference's open airy composition: keep horizon in UPPER THIRD about 32% from TOP, strip of wooded opposite bank, tiny distant white church on far right. At very top left ONLY a few leaves, most sky clear for layering animated clouds later. Tasteful soft clear sky, leave cloud sprites to game engine. Clouds should be very sparse and subtle if any. No fishing tackle, overlays, frames, fish, boats, people, birds, signs, lettering or numbers. 2x2 atmospheric plates, same sandy bank position in every quadrant; no warm evening or night tint on the whole sheet. High detail for a small realistic game, landscape sheet.

## Берег для динамического освещения

```text
Precisely edit this 2x2 atlas of four views at the same Volga sandy fishing bank. Retain exact same geometry, left sandy bank, forest, horizon, relative positioning and FOUR different original atmospheres, morning mist upper left, blue daylight upper right, russet evening lower left, cool night lower right. In the upper left and lower left panels completely REMOVE the sun disc, its halo and ALL directional specular/glitter paths on the water. Reconstruct smooth continuous sky, distant tree line and ordinary rippled river. These are background plates for a game renderer that adds the moving sun AND the reflection separately. Morning ambient is uniformly pale rose with no yellow focal patch. Evening ambient is muted diffuse amber and lavender with no bright center, no highlights, no shafts. No direct sunlight on the bank in either. Midday neutral. Night may KEEP the moon and its reflection, softly fading moon. Four tiles remain distinct pictures. No objects, UI or text. Attached image is ONLY edit target, preserve grid with no gutters/dividers.
```

## Солнечная дорожка

За основу взяты [закат на Волге в Самаре](https://pikabu.ru/story/zakat_na_volge_samara_9348814) и [фотография отражения в ряби](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-fire-on-the-water-K0r1Jb98tss).

Production RGBA sprite for sun glitter on a gently rippled river. Two reference photos: study the column of sparkling wave crests under the sun. Generate ONLY glints with genuine per-pixel transparency, photorealistic muted pale amber and white specks, no sky, no sun disc, no water plane, no horizon. A STRAIGHT VERTICAL COLUMN of approximately CONSTANT perceived width all the way from top to bottom. Do not converge to a point or fan outward. In upper rows many tiny tightly spaced muted horizontal flecks; middle and lower rows progress to larger, irregular, broken short highlights separated by transparent gaps, with the same centerline and the same overall width as the upper rows. Keep generous empty lateral margins, falloff on the sides is stochastic and very soft; stray glints fade gradually; no solid lines marking edges. Subtly fade at the very top and bottom. No fog, no glowing trapezoid, no checkerboard. Vertical composition. Aim for the actual visual distribution of the supplied reference photos but output only the reflected light as a photographic VFX layer.
